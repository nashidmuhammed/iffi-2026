57th IFFI 2026 to open with 'Better Man' by Australian filmmaker Michael Gracey in Goa

Opening Ceremony to celebrate India's cultural and cinematic heritage with a star-studded gala at Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Stadium

The 57th edition of the International Film Festival of India (IFFI 2026) is all set to commence with a glittering Opening Ceremony at Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Stadium in Goa, on November 20, 2026 at 5:00 PM. The festivities will begin with the Red-Carpet World Premiere of the Opening Film – 'Better Man', directed by acclaimed Australian filmmaker Michael Gracey, at INOX Panjim at 2:00 PM.

Opening Film Premiere & Dignitaries

The Opening Film Premiere will be attended by Union Minister of Information and Broadcasting, Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw along with Minister of State for Information & Broadcasting, Dr. L. Murugan. On the occasion, Chief Minister of Goa, Dr. Pramod Sawant will extend a warm welcome to international and Indian guests to the scenic coastal state of Goa, where IFFI has found its permanent home since 2004.

The presence of these dignitaries along with the production team and cast of 'Better Man' underscores the significance of the festival as a cultural and cinematic celebration of global importance, bridging traditions and modern storytelling under the overarching theme "Stories Without Borders".

"IFFI 2026 stands as an emblem of cinema's universal power to unite cultures, spark dialogue, and showcase India's burgeoning creative economy to the world."
— Ministry of Information & Broadcasting, Government of India

Star-Studded Opening Ceremony

The gala opening ceremony, amidst the presence of cinematic legends, promises to deliver an unforgettable evening of cultural performances that will remain etched in the hearts of cinema lovers for years to come.

The Opening Ceremony will be hosted by popular film personalities Abhishek Banerjee and Bhumi Pednekar. The grand evening will bring together the best of Indian cinema as the 57th IFFI begins its week-long journey of cinematic excellence from November 20 to 28, 2026. The ceremony will witness the participation of numerous celebrated directors, actors, cinematographers, and technicians whose presence will set the tone for the international festival.

Iconic filmmakers and creators including Subhash Ghai, Dinesh Vijan, Amar Kaushik, NM Suresh, RK Selvamani, Ishari Ganeshan, Ravi Kotarakara, and lyricist Prasoon Joshi will grace the Opening Ceremony. Renowned actors Nagarjuna, Nitya Menon, Amala Akkineni, Vikrant Massey, Rakul Preet Singh, Manushi Chhillar, Boman Irani, Rajkummar Rao, Jaideep Ahlawat, Randeep Hooda, Sanya Malhotra, Jayam Ravi, Jackky Bhagnani, R. Sarath Kumar, Mukta Barve, Sonalee Kulkarni, and Radhakrishnan Parthiban will also be in attendance alongside global film delegations.

The stellar lineup also includes spiritual leader Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, who will inspire attendees with a special keynote address on the role of storytelling and peaceful harmony through arts.

Special Focus: Australia

This year’s 57th edition features Australia as the Country of Focus. A specially curated package of contemporary and classic Australian cinema will be showcased during the festival week. The Jannawi Dance Clan, an acclaimed Australian First Nations dance group, will present an enthralling cultural performance at the opening gala, fostering vibrant bilateral cultural exchange and indigenous narrative art.

In addition, the Accessible IFFI initiative ensures that all major ceremonies and screenings feature live Indian Sign Language (ISL) interpretation, audio descriptions, and wheelchair-accessible infrastructure across venues in Panaji and Porvorim.
